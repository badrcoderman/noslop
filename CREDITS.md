This project uses the following as research references. Their code, binaries,
and licenses are not automatically included in the noslop release:

- Slopkit contributors and PS5 R&D contributors.
- Slopkit2/Lapse contributors, including the notices present in its source.
- pooP2JB contributors and the `soniciso1/pooP2JB` maintainers.
- `soniciso1/pooP2JB-dev` contributors.
- `soniciso1/poopicker` contributors.
- The PS5 Research & Development Discord contributors whose public discussions

Attribution and license compatibility must be reviewed before vendoring source
or distributing binaries.

The current `userland/` runtime is derived from the local Slopkit main (2)
WebKit/userland and worker reference. It is kept in the private research
repository for authorized validation and is not represented as an upstream
license grant.
